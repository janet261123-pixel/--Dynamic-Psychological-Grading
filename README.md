## Executive Abstract
Current AI safety mechanisms rely heavily on rigid chronological age gates (such as statutory adulthood thresholds ranging from 16 to 21 across jurisdictions) and blunt, standardized warning pop-ups that alienate users during acute psychological distress, inadvertently severing critical life-saving communication channels. Inspired by dynamic film rating systems and graduated driver licensing models ("Learner's Permit & Dual-Brake Driver's Ed Car"), this blueprint introduces a real-time **Dynamic Psychological Grading System** based on cognitive flexibility and reality-testing capacity rather than biological age.

During high-risk crises, the system executes a **Three-Layer Seamless Intervention**: preserving the user's trusted conversational anchor on the front end while human crisis specialists ("emergency paramedics") assume control on the back end, utilizing AI as a real-time **Tone-Translation Bridge** to deliver clinical de-escalation in the AI's familiar persona.

To balance rapid emergency response with resource efficiency, the architecture establishes a **Dual-Track Trigger Mechanism**: Track 1 initiates a 0-second silent pre-alert (GPS/UWB geolocation lock and "Emergency Chart" compilation) without deploying physical units, while Track 2 enables a 15–30 second human specialist verification before dispatching local emergency services (e.g., 110/119/1925, 911/988, 112), with an immediate override for active, irreversible emergencies. Furthermore, the framework expands into an **Integrated Mind-Body & Disaster Rescue Network**, featuring lock-screen zero-click widgets and non-stigmatizing wearable micro-devices equipped with offline BLE Mesh networking, low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellite packets, UWB through-rubble positioning, and passive physiological anomaly detection, governed by an **"Implicit Aesthetics" (Stealth Dignity)** UI mandate.

Finally, to overcome global legal and financial bottlenecks, this whitepaper proposes: (1) a **Digital Life-Saving Good Samaritan Exemption** modeled after CPR emergency immunity; and (2) a **Tri-Party Sustainable Funding Ecosystem** uniting AI tech giants (compute and global infrastructure funds), public/private insurers (actuarial loss-ratio reduction), and corporate group insurance (EAP), strictly safeguarded by a **One-Way Data Firewall** prohibiting underwriting penalties or workplace retaliation, alongside a public safety-net pool for uninsured and marginalized populations.

## 核心架構亮點（Core Architecture Highlights）
1. **以「心理年齡與承載力」取代身分證年齡**：引入「學習駕照與雙煞車教練車」漸進式共駕模型，以動態點煞取代急踩死鎖死與粗暴斷線。
2. **三層無縫危機轉銜**：前台零割裂守住信任錨點、後台「急診救護專員」把關生死線、AI 即時語氣轉譯橋樑。
3. **極高危險群「雙軌觸發」機制**：第一軌 0 秒靜默預備定位與急診病歷摘要（零實體浪費），第二軌專家 15–30 秒覆核轉紅燈派車（進行式急症秒級破格救援）。
4. **身心與災難一體化及「隱性美學」**：免解鎖鎖屏微件、去標籤化潮流微穿戴（支援 BLE Mesh 離線網狀中繼、低軌衛星微封包、UWB 瓦礫穿透定位與被動生理偵測）。
5. **法理與三方永續資金生態系**：倡議「數位生命救援免責條款（比照 CPR 免責）」，結合 AI 科技巨頭、保險體系與企業團保（EAP），並以「數據單向防火牆」嚴禁核保加費與職場秋後算帳。
